FAQs

What services do BetterPay Services provide?

BetterPay Services offers registration for domestic workers, including cleaners, carers, gardeners, and nursing professionals. We also assist businesses with staffing and administrative needs.

How can I register as a work seeker?

You can register as a work seeker by providing necessary documents such as police clearance, ID, and competence certificates. You may also upload your availability directly on our platform.

Is there a fee for using BetterPay Services?

Clients can browse the website for free. To access detailed profiles of work seekers, registration is required, which may involve a nominal fee based on the services you select.

How do clients select candidates for interview?

Once registered, clients can view work seekers' names and qualifications. They can select up to three candidates for interviews, and those candidates will receive the client's contact information.

What happens if my availability changes?

It is crucial to keep your availability updated. If selected for a gig based on outdated availability, there may be penalties imposed on your account.

What payment options are available through BetterPay Services?

Clients can choose to pay employees directly or subscribe to BetterPay Services' payment options, which include monthly salaries, leave provisions, and contributions to medical aid and pension.
