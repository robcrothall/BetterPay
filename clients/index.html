FAQ

How do I register as a client with BetterPay Services?

To register as a client, simply visit our registration page, fill out your details, and submit. You'll gain access to our full range of services and can start browsing our available work-seekers immediately!

What types of workers can I find through BetterPay Services?

You can find a variety of workers including domestic staff like cleaners, cooks, and gardeners, as well as nursing professionals and independent contractors. Browse our categories to see what's available.

What if my selected work-seeker doesn't work out?

If the interview with your selected work-seeker isn't successful, feel free to choose up to three more candidates to interview until you find the perfect fit for your needs!

How does the payment process work?

Once you've selected a work-seeker, you have the option to either pay them directly or subscribe to our payroll services. We will handle the calculations, provide payslips, and invoice you monthly to simplify the process.
